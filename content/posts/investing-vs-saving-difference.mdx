Saving protects money you need soon. Investing grows money you won't need for a while. Getting this wrong costs real money through missed growth or sudden losses.

## What Saving Actually Means
Saving is setting money aside in a stable, accessible place like a high-yield savings account or CD. 
* **Primary Goal:** Protect money from risk.
* **Returns:** Typically 4% to 5% annually in high-yield accounts.
* **Risk:** Very low, often FDIC-insured up to $250,000.
* **Best Use:** Emergency funds and short-term goals under three years.

## What Investing Actually Means
Investing means putting money into assets like stocks, bonds, index funds, or ETFs that can grow over time but carry short-term market risk.
* **Primary Goal:** Grow money over the long term.
* **Returns:** Historically 8% to 10% annually over long stretches.
* **Risk:** Moderate to high in the short term, with no guarantees.
* **Best Use:** Retirement and long-term goals five or more years away.

## When to Save vs When to Invest
* **Save When:** You are building an emergency fund, saving for a home down payment within the next few years, or holding money you cannot afford to lose.
* **Invest When:** You are building wealth for retirement, funding goals more than five years out, or your emergency fund is already established.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I save or invest first?
Save first, at least enough to cover a small emergency (one to three months of expenses), then shift additional money toward long-term investing.

### Is a high-yield savings account considered investing?
No. High-yield savings accounts offer stability and better interest rates than traditional accounts, but they lack market risk and true growth potential.

## Key Takeaways
Saving protects your short-term present, while investing builds your long-term future. Most people need both tools working simultaneously for different financial goals.

## What To Do Next
Evaluate your current cash distribution. If your emergency fund is thin, prioritize high-yield savings; if your safety net is solid, direct your extra funds toward long-term investing.
