Your inbox has an alert from a company you've never used, telling you your information was part of a data breach. Freezing your credit is a free process that takes about ten minutes per bureau and stops anyone, including you, from opening new credit in your name until you unfreeze it.

## What a Credit Freeze Actually Does
A credit freeze restricts access to your credit report. When a lender tries to pull your report to approve a new credit card or loan, a frozen file blocks that request entirely. No access means no approval, closing the door on fraudulent new accounts without affecting your existing credit cards or loans.

## What a Freeze Doesn't Protect Against
* **Existing accounts:** A freeze won't prevent fraudulent charges on credit cards you already hold.
* **Tax or medical fraud:** Someone filing a fraudulent tax return or using your insurance for medical services doesn't require a credit check.
* **Credit score impact:** A freeze has zero impact on your credit score.

## Credit Freeze vs. Credit Lock vs. Fraud Alert
* **Credit Freeze:** Free, governed by federal law, available directly through each bureau, and requires a PIN or password to lift.
* **Credit Lock:** Offered by bureaus as a paid add-on or subscription, allowing you to toggle access via an app.
* **Fraud Alert:** Requires lenders to verify your identity before extending new credit rather than blocking access outright.

## How to Freeze Your Credit: Step by Step
1. **Gather Your Information:** Have your full name, date of birth, Social Security number, and current address ready.
2. **Freeze With All Three Bureaus:** You must freeze your files separately with Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion.
3. **Save Your PINs:** Store your PINs or passwords securely, as you will need them to lift the freeze later.
4. **Consider Children's Credit:** All three bureaus allow parents to request a freeze for a minor's credit file to prevent misuse of a Social Security number.

## How to Lift or Remove a Credit Freeze
You can temporarily lift a freeze for a specific date range or a specific lender when you are ready to apply for a mortgage, auto loan, or new credit card. Most bureaus process temporary lifts instantly online using your PIN or password.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it free to freeze my credit?
Yes. Federal law requires that credit freezes be free at all three bureaus, with no cost to place, lift, or remove one.

### Does freezing my credit hurt my score?
No. A freeze has zero effect on your credit score and only blocks new account openings while active.

## Key Takeaways
Freezing your credit takes minutes, costs nothing, and provides immediate protection against new account fraud. Remember to freeze all three bureaus separately and keep your PINs in a secure place.
